========================================--------------------
Early Documentations (circa 2023) of what Nehemiah was planned to be devleloped like.
Most ideas ended up sticking, but the characters were changed to better fit their own niches and require a lot less custom callbacks
========================================--------------------

Nehemiah
Starts with: Nehemiah’s Hammer (PA) (Latent), Shiny Rock
When Nehemiah holds his hammer, instead of pickups, rocks drop chunks that can be thrown at enemies, but when carved a second time, the rock is destroyed and drops 1-2 pickups or rock spiders

Mom’s Heart ✡️ - The Masons //familliar !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
“Your Personal Wrecking Crew”
— Spawns a familiar that changes every floor, can be: A tiny Nehemiah that shoots nail tears, A tiny mason that shoots brick tears, A tiny buff mason that bounces around the room and shoots a tiny spike ball at nearby enemies, and an old man that shoots rock tears at nearby enemies that split into more tears.

Isaac - Happy Hour - 1 //active -----XXXXXX-----
“Benefits of Nobility”
— For the current room, gives Isaac an All Stats Up (+10%) and a random worm trinket effect.

Satan - Golden Apple //passive -----XXXXX-----
“The Lord Returns”
— Extra Life (x1); Respawn with 10 seconds of invincibility and 10 seconds of Midas’ touch
:-- Updated Effect --:
— Right when Isaac would take fatal damage, grants a half soul heart, a golden heart, and ~2 seconds of invincibility and Midas Touch

??? - Holy Smokes! //passive -----XXXXX-----
“Return of the Torch” / "It's Called Aura, Mom"
— Gives Isaac a small Orange aura that burns enemies inside; +20% Angel Chance

The Lamb - Woolen Blanket //passive -----XXXXX-----
“The Lord Comforts”
— The first damage taken on a floor is reduced to half a heart of damage, and after being hit Isaac has extra long invincibility; Luck Up (+2)

Mega Satan - Nehemiah’s Hammer //active -----XXXXX-----
“I’m Gonna Wreck It!”
— Works similarly to Notched Axe, but instead of breaking rocks, carves them into a funny shape with a chance to drop a goodie or two; Using this item on a carved rock will destroy it, which yields a greater chance to drop goodies.
— Tinted Rocks are guaranteed to drop a pickup when carved, and tinted rock spiders upon destruction
— The Hammer can open any doors that would normally require bombs to enter, such as Secret Rooms, Paneled Doors, and the entrance to the Mines
— — Picking up Iron Bar will fully recharge this item
: -- Updated Effect --:
— Works like Notched Axe, though when you destroy a rock, there's a high chance for rock chunks to descend from the ceiling, TR Samson style
— Isaac can pick up the rocks and throw them at enemies, doing substantial damage and splitting into 3 rock tears on collision
— The Item was changed to be timer-based, so the Iron Bar no longer recharges it.

Boss Rush - Dumb Luck //trinket -----XXXXX-----
“Foolish Wishes” //Windflower

Hush - Oily Branch //trinket -----XXXXX-----
“Smells like Olives”
— Isaac’s tears have a chance to charm enemies

Delirium - The Memoir //passive
“Take a Deep Breath”

— Tears Down (-0.4)
— 3 Random Stats Up (+15%)
— Isaac gains a permanent tear-repelling aura, similar to I - The Magician?

Mother - Butterfly Wings (52 pattern) //trinket -----XXXXX-----
“I Feel Good!”
— Higher Chance for a room to contain a tinted rock; The chance of this happening is based on your luck stat.

The Beast - Old Brick //passive -----XXXXX-----

“Feel the Mercy”

:-- Updated Effect --:

Ultra Greedier 🪙 - Graceful Charity //card -----XXXXX-----
“You Win Some, You Lose Some”
— Destroys 2 random items you possess, and spawns 3 items from the current room’s item pool

All Marks ✡️ - Cement Hearts //pickup -----XXXXX-----
“Definitely bad for you”
— Cement Hearts act as heart armor, like golden hearts, except they cover your left-most heart and take 4 damage to destroy. They are also immediately destroyed when Isaac takes explosion damage

T. Nehemiah
Starts with: Book of Ezra (PA) (Latent), Pistanthrophobia, Buried Secret Rooms
Also: When playing as T. Nehemiah, Walls connecting a Room with a Secret Room will stand out in some way, but will also take 2 Bombs to open
:-- Updated Effect --:
— When Playing as T. Nehemiah, Secret Rooms will be given new visible doors that require 2 bombs to be opened
— Secret Rooms have become quite a hot topic...
— Using the Book of Ezra in a shop will attempt to open the Member Card shop, though you should remember that your actions have consequences...
